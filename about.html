Our Identity

Who We Are

R&P SPORTS is a dedicated apparel manufacturing enterprise located on Chirawa Road, Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan, India. Founded with the mission to provide dependable, precision-engineered garment production, we operate as a full-service B2B manufacturing partner for athletic clubs, apparel brands, retailers, and corporate organizations.

Our production facility is equipped to handle comprehensive sportswear, activewear, training apparel, and specialized uniform manufacturing requirements with unwavering attention to stitch density, fit ergonomics, and durable finishing.

Operations

Our Manufacturing Approach

At R&P SPORTS, we believe that high-grade apparel requires systematic discipline at every stage. We do not cut corners on material inspection or thread quality.

Our approach centers on close collaboration with our clients: understanding their technical requirements, reviewing sizing grade rules, inspecting fabric rolls before cutting, and enforcing multi-stage quality checkpoints before packaging.

By blending modern industrial machinery with skilled textile craftsmanship, we deliver garments that help our clients build strong brand reputations in their respective markets.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable garment manufacturing solutions with consistent quality, dependable delivery schedules, and professional service tailored to the specific requirements of our business partners.

Our Vision

To build enduring, trust-based relationships with sports brands, businesses, retailers, and organizations across India through dependable, high-integrity apparel manufacturing.
