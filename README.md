# R&P SPORTS — Corporate Garment Manufacturing Website

**R&P SPORTS** is a premier B2B garment manufacturing company based on Chirawa Road, Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan, India (+91 96721 45942).

This repository contains the complete 10-page luxury corporate website designed for high-end garment manufacturing, sportswear, activewear, and custom bulk apparel production.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- **Luxury Industrial Aesthetic**: Deep charcoal (`#090B0E`), metallic slate, white, and logo-matched brand sapphire blue (`#1E5DD6`).
- **10 Complete Pages**:
  1. `index.html` — Homepage with 3-second automatic hero slideshow, capabilities, product showcase, 6-stage process, and CTA.
  2. `about.html` — Corporate profile, Chirawa Road Jhunjhunu heritage, mission, vision, and 6 core values.
  3. `manufacturing.html` — Detailed 6-stage production workflow and industrial apparel operations.
  4. `products.html` — Comprehensive garment collection catalog featuring real product photos and interactive category filters.
  5. `quality.html` — Quality assurance pillars, seam testing, and zero-defect quality promise.
  6. `infrastructure.html` — Modern factory production floor, cutting tables, stitching lines, and dispatch hub.
  7. `custom-manufacturing.html` — Custom OEM/ODM apparel solutions, sampling workflow, and interactive RFQ form.
  8. `gallery.html` — Editorial photo gallery with category filters and interactive full-screen lightbox modal.
  9. `why-choose-us.html` — B2B partner advantages, reliability, bulk production support, and long-term vision.
  10. `contact.html` — Contact info, interactive quotation form with instant WhatsApp integration, direct phone call triggers, and map location.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Floating pulse WhatsApp button on every page + instant pre-filled order inquiry links for product and quote forms (`+91 96721 45942`).
- **Mobile Responsive**: Fully responsive across mobile devices, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide displays.
- **SEO Optimized**: Title tags, meta descriptions, semantic HTML5, and Schema.org JSON-LD LocalBusiness markup.
