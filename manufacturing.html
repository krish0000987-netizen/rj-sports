Manufacturing Capability

From Raw Material to Finished Garment — Precision Engineered at Every Stage.

End-to-End Workflow

A Disciplined Production Ecosystem

Our manufacturing floor is systematically organized to maintain clean transitions between textile handling, pattern cutting, machine stitching, quality verification, and dispatch.

Stage 01

Fabric Preparation & Inspection

The quality of any high-performance garment begins with its raw material. We source athletic knits, breathable mesh, poly-blends, and custom fabrics according to client requirements.

Each batch is checked for weave consistency, uniform shade, weight distribution, and absence of textile flaws prior to being staged for cutting.

  • Fabric tension relaxation before layout
  • Defect screening and lot sorting
Stage 02

Pattern Grading & Precision Cutting

Accurate sizing depends on exacting pattern design and calibrated cutting. Master pattern sets are graded to standard industry size scales or tailored client specifications.

Multi-ply fabric spreading allows our operators to achieve millimeter precision across bulk panels, ensuring consistent armhole curves, chest fits, and hem alignments.

  • Accurate size grading from Small to 3XL+
  • Clean edge cutting preventing fraying
Stage 03

Industrial Stitching & Assembly Lines

Our sewing lines are staffed by experienced apparel technicians operating heavy-duty single-needle, overlock, and flatlock industrial sewing machinery.

Garments are assembled in progressive sections: collar ribbing, sleeve attachment, side-seam overlocking, and hem finishing — reinforced for athletic durability and intense physical movement.

  • High-strength multi-thread overlock seams
  • Consistent stitches per inch (SPI)
Stage 04

Rigorous Quality Inspection

Quality control technicians inspect 100% of finished garments under dedicated high-lumen workstations. Every piece is examined against specification sheets.

We verify key parameters including chest width, total length, sleeve symmetry, stitch stability, and label placement.

  • Dimensional tolerance verification
  • Seam stretch and strength testing
Stage 05

Finishing, Thread Trimming & Pressing

Post-inspection, garments move to the finishing line for loose thread trimming, steam pressing, and final shape setting.

Steam pressing removes wrinkles and gives the activewear a crisp, retail-ready presentation, while keeping the garment fibers supple.

  • Thorough loose-thread suction & manual clipping
  • Industrial steam pressing and aesthetic touch-up
Stage 06

Protective Packing & Dispatch

The completed order is folded, polybagged, and organized by size ratios into heavy-duty corrugated cartons designed for long-distance road and rail transport across India.

Cartons are sealed, labeled with SKU codes, piece counts, and customer addresses to ensure hassle-free warehouse check-in for our B2B partners.

  • Individual protective polybags
  • Heavy-duty corrugated master cartons
