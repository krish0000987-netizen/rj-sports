Step-by-Step Workflow

Our 6-Stage Manufacturing Process

A structured and disciplined workflow from raw fabric receipt to dispatch-ready packaging.

01

Fabric & Material Selection

Thorough checking of incoming fabric rolls for weave consistency, texture, and suitability for the required garment type.

02

Pattern & Precision Cutting

Accurate pattern plotting and multi-layer fabric cutting ensuring precise dimensions across all size sets.

03

Stitching & Assembly

Skilled garment technicians assemble panels using industrial sewing machines with durable stitch tension.

04

Quality Inspection

Comprehensive visual and dimensional checks to verify seam alignment, measurements, and finishing accuracy.

05

Finishing

Thread trimming, steam pressing, and final touch-ups to ensure a polished presentation.

06

Packing & Dispatch

Individual polybag packaging, size-wise sorting, and heavy-duty carton packing for safe transit.
