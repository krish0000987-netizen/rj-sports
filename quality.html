Our Standard

The 5 Pillars of R&P Quality

We structure our production checkpoints to identify and address any discrepancy before garments reach the packaging phase.

Quality-Focused Manufacturing

Workmanship is treated as a priority across all assembly lines. Operators adhere to standardized tension, thread types, and needle gauges to prevent seam slippage.

Fabric & Material Attention

We carefully match fabric weight, stretch recovery, and breathability to the client's intended application — whether high-mobility sports or durable uniforms.

Stitching & Construction

Reinforced bar-tacking on stress points, uniform stitches-per-inch (SPI), and neat overlocked seams protect garments from premature fraying or tearing.

Finishing Inspection

Each piece is checked against critical measurements: chest circumference, neck drop, body length, armhole curve, and sleeve hem symmetry.

Batch Consistency

Consistency is vital for B2B apparel brands. We maintain uniform patterns and sewing templates so piece 1 and piece 1,000 match seamlessly.

Zero Compromise

Defective panels or sub-standard seams are rejected immediately and re-sewn prior to moving forward in the workflow.
