Production Environment

Well-illuminated, ventilated factory floor organized in dedicated modular cells to enable continuous flow and minimize handling damage.

Heavy-duty fabric spreading tables and precision pattern cutting machinery calibrated for clean edges across bulk fabric plies.

Dedicated industrial steam pressing tables, thread-cleaning units, and folding stations ensuring retail-standard garment appearance.

Organized staging and cartonization area with barcode-ready carton labeling for smooth logistics dispatch across Indian transport corridors.
